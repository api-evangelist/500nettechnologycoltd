# 500net Technology Co., Ltd.

> 500net Technology Co., Ltd. (五百戶科技股份有限公司) is a Taipei-based systems integrator founded in
> 2004. It combines telecommunications integration, electromechanical engineering, hardware design
> and custom software development into smart-building, smart-factory, smart-parking and AI+IoT
> platforms for enterprise clients in Taiwan. As of 2026-09-05 the company publishes NO public API,
> developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable contract of any kind — integration is delivered as a
> project engagement, and device-level interoperability is described in its own materials as
> RS-485 / Modbus, wired, wireless and 4G/5G controller integration rather than a web API.

## Solutions
- [Smart Building](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/Smart_Building.html): BA central-monitoring, green-building certification support, scheduled scenario control.
- [Smart Factory](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/Smart_Factory.html): Industry 4.0 production reporting, power monitoring, AI/AOI inspection, AMR robotics.
- [Smart Parking](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/Smart_Parking.html): plate recognition, card and mobile payment integration, municipal parking system integration.
- [Heterogeneous Integration](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/Heterogeneous_Integration.html): multi-vendor controller and device integration over 485/Modbus, wired, wireless, 4G/5G.
- [Customization](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/Customization.html): contract software development and IT outsourcing.
- [Carbon Footprint / ESG](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/CFV.html): carbon inventory and ESG reporting.
- [Digital Twins](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/Digital_Twins.html)
- [Products](https://www.500net.com.tw/tw/Product.html)
